Natural Winter-run Chinook

Juvenile Production Estimate

The Juvenile Production Estimate for winter-run is 1,057,452 for the current water year.

Current Status

Red Bluff Diversion Dam Passage Estimate - As of Jan 28 estimated passage to date of LAD winter-run at Red Bluff Diversion is approximately 4.17 million fish. Note that outmigration timing overlaps with spring-run migrating fish, and true winter-run abundance likely differs from these estimates.

Total catch at Sacramento Trawl and Beach Seines in the delta between Jan 20 and Jan 30 is 2 individuals. Total catch at Delta Exit at Chipps Island between Jan 20 and Jan 30 is 0 individuals.

Annual Loss

The annual loss threshold for natural winter-run is 1% of the JPE or 10,575 fish. The single-year incidental take limit (ITL) is 0.56% of the JPE (5,922 fish) or 0.36% on a 3-year rolling average (BiOp Table 184). As of February 01, cumulative loss of genetically confirmed winter-run is 0 or 0.00% of the annual loss threshold. Cumulative loss in the past 7 days has been 0.

STARS

The Delta STARS Model is an individual-based simulation model that predicts survival, travel time, and routing of juvenile salmon migrating through the Sacramento–San Joaquin River Delta. This model gives insight into survival and routing patterns of winter-run based on most current conditions.

As of February 01, overall through delta STARS estimated survival probability (with 80% credible intervals) is 0.6 (0.54-0.66) placing it in the 71st percentile of historical STARS survival estimates for the month of February (WYs 2018-2025). STARS estimated routing and survival probabilities (with 80% credible intervals) into the interior delta are 0.12 (0.1-0.14) and 0.32 (0.19-0.46), respectively, corresponding to the 38th and 62nd percentiles of historical February estimates (WYs 2018-2025).
