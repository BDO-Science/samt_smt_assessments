STARS

The Delta STARS Model is an individual-based simulation model that predicts survival, travel time, and routing of juvenile salmon migrating through the Sacramento–San Joaquin River Delta. This model gives insight into survival and routing patterns of winter-run based on most current conditions.

As of January 07, overall through delta STARS estimated survival probability (with 80% credible intervals) is 0.71 (0.62-0.8) placing it in the 91st percentile of historical STARS survival estimates for the month of January (WYs 2018-2025). STARS estimated routing and survival probabilities (with 80% credible intervals) into the interior delta are 0.01 (0-0.02) and 0.8 (0.7-0.89), respectively, corresponding to the 2nd and 96th percentiles of historical January estimates (WYs 2018-2025).
