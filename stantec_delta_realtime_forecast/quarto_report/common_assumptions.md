The model run results cover the period `r fmt_date(forecast_start_date)` through `r fmt_date(forecast_end_date)` and are based on the following assumptions established by DWR: 
1.	CCFB Gates are operating to Priority 1. 
2.	The Delta Cross Channel gates are currently closed. Starting May 22, they are open on weekends and closed on weekdays.
3.	Suisun Marsh Salinity Control flashboards are in. All three gates are in open position from March 2.
4.	San Joaquin River flow at Vernalis is at 1939 cfs at the beginning of the forecast period and is estimated to decrease to 1450 cfs by the end of the forecast period.
5.	San Joaquin River EC at Vernalis is at 240 umhos/cm at the beginning of the forecast period and is estimated to increase to 324 umhos/cm at the end of the forecast period.
6.	Sacramento River flow at Freeport is at 8,844 cfs at the beginning of the forecast period and is expected to increase to 11,000 cfs by the end of the forecast period.
7.	CCFB inflow is at 597 cfs at the beginning of the forecast period and is expected to increase to 1,000 cfs by the end of the forecast period. 
8.	Export at Jones Pumping Plant is at 894 cfs at the beginning of the forecast period and is expected to increase to 900 cfs to the end of the forecast period. 
9.	The Middle River barrier is closed on May 5, and will be in intermediate culvert operation starting June 1 and full operation starting June 15. 
10.	The Old River near Tracy barrier was closed on May 22, and will be in intermediate culvert operation starting June 1 and full operation starting June 15. 
11.	The Grant Line Canal is closed on June 4, and will be in intermediate culvert operation starting June 5 and full operation starting June 15.
As shown in the next section, assumptions 4 and 6 are updated based on Reclamation forecast data, and assumption 8 and exports at Banks Pumping Plant have been modified to include four different forecast scenarios at an Old and Middle River (OMR) index of -6,500 cfs, -5,000 cfs, -3,500 cfs, and -2,000 cfs.
