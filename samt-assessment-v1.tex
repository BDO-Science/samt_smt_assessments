\subsection{Assessment for Delta Operations on
Salmonids}\label{assessment-for-delta-operations-on-salmonids}

Last updated: \emph{March 18, 2026}

For more detailed data on salmonid conditions in the Delta see
corresponding webpage on
\href{https://www.cbr.washington.edu/sacramento/workgroups/salmon_monitoring.html}{SacPAS}.

\subsubsection{Executive Summary}\label{executive-summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  Entrainment management season is active.
\item
  Annual Loss: 4 (0.03\% of annual loss threshold) natural winter-run,
  46 (3.51\% of annual loss threshold) hatchery winter-run (Sac River),
  2 (4.47\% of annual loss threshold) hatchery winter-run (Battle
  Creek), 210 natural steelhead, 1411 (23.11\% of annual loss threshold)
  hatchery steelhead, and 1058 (48.12\% of annual loss threshold)
  spring-run surrogates.
\item
  Single-year Incidental Take Limit (ITL) Status: 4 (0.07\% of 5,922
  ITL) natural winter-run; 46 (3.54\% of 1,301 ITL) hatchery winter-run
  (Sac River); 2 (3.85\% of 52 ITL) hatchery winter-run (Battle Creek);
  210 (3.97\% of 5,294 ITL) natural steelhead.
\item
  Spring-run surrogate yearlings (0.5\% ITL per experimental release
  group): Group 1: 0 (0\% of 376 ITL); Group 2: 257 (84.64\% of 304
  ITL); Group 3: 35 (12.26\% of 286 ITL).
\item
  LAD winter-run presence in the Delta is high based on historical
  monitoring.
\item
  Steelhead presence in the Delta is high based on historical
  monitoring.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{STARS}\label{stars}

The Delta STARS Model is an individual-based simulation model that
predicts survival, travel time, and routing of juvenile salmon migrating
through the Sacramento--San Joaquin River Delta. This model gives
insight into survival and routing patterns of winter-run based on most
current conditions.

As of March 17, overall through delta STARS estimated survival
probability (with 80\% credible intervals) is 0.26 (0.19-0.34) placing
it in the 17th percentile of historical STARS survival estimates for the
month of March (WYs 2018-2025). STARS estimated routing and survival
probabilities (with 80\% credible intervals) into the interior delta are
0.13 (0.1-0.15) and 0.11 (0.05-0.2), respectively, corresponding to the
58th and 42nd percentiles of historical March estimates (WYs 2018-2025).
